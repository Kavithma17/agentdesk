# Delivery Policy – Lanka Gadgets

## Delivery Charges

| Area | Charge | Delivery Time |
|---|---|---|
| Colombo and suburbs (Colombo, Dehiwala, Nugegoda, Maharagama, Kotte, Wattala) | Rs. 350 | 1–2 working days |
| Other districts in Western, Central, Southern, Sabaragamuwa, North Western, North Central and Uva provinces (e.g. Kandy, Galle, Kurunegala) | Rs. 450 | 2–4 working days |
| Northern and Eastern provinces (e.g. Jaffna, Trincomalee, Batticaloa) | Rs. 550 | 3–5 working days |

**Free delivery** on all orders above Rs. 15,000, island-wide.

## Order Processing

- Orders placed before 2:00 PM on a working day are dispatched the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched the next working day.
- You will receive an SMS with a tracking number once your order is dispatched.

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

- Cash on delivery is available for orders up to Rs. 50,000.
- Orders above Rs. 50,000 must be paid in advance by card or bank transfer.

If your order has not arrived within the delivery time above, contact us with your order number. We will check with our courier partner and reply within one working day.
