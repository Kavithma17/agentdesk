# Frequently Asked Questions – Lanka Gadgets

## About Us

Lanka Gadgets is an online store selling phone accessories, audio products and wearables. We deliver island-wide across Sri Lanka.

## Contact and Opening Hours

- Customer support hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
- We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.
- Messages received outside support hours are answered the next working day.

## Payment Methods

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard)
- Bank transfer
- Cash on delivery (for orders up to Rs. 50,000)
- 3-month interest-free installments on selected credit cards, for orders above Rs. 20,000

## How do I track my order?

After your order is dispatched, you receive an SMS with a tracking number. You can also ask our support agent for your order status using your order number. Order numbers look like `LG-10234`.

## Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change or cancel your order free of charge **before it is dispatched**. Once dispatched, it cannot be cancelled, but you may be able to return it under our returns policy.

## Do you have a physical shop?

No. We are online only, which helps us keep prices low.

## Are your products genuine?

Yes. All Nova products are sold with an official warranty and an invoice.
