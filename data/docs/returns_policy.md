# Returns, Refunds and Warranty – Lanka Gadgets

## Change of Mind Returns

- You can return an item within **7 days** of delivery if you changed your mind.
- The item must be **unopened**, in its original sealed packaging, with all accessories.
- The customer pays the return delivery charge.
- Opened items cannot be returned for change of mind.

## Defective or Wrong Items

- If an item is defective on arrival, or we sent the wrong item, report it within **14 days** of delivery.
- Send a photo or short video of the problem along with your order number.
- We collect the item free of charge and send a replacement. If no replacement is in stock, we give a full refund.

## Refunds

- Approved refunds are processed within **5–7 working days**.
- Card payments are refunded to the same card.
- Cash on delivery and bank transfer orders are refunded by bank transfer. We will ask for your bank account details.

## Warranty

- Each product has its own warranty period, listed in the product catalog.
- Warranty covers manufacturing defects only. It does not cover physical damage, water damage, or damage from incorrect chargers.
- To make a warranty claim, contact us with your order number and a description of the problem. Keep your invoice.
